I. Before we knew

Two roll numbers in the same classroom

We went to the same school. That's it. Not friends barely acquaintances. Just two names that happened to know the other one existed, the way you know a face in a corridor without ever learning its story.

There was a rule: every roll number took a turn cleaning the classroom in the second break. The day it was mine, you were the one supervising. And you made me do it twice. No idea, back then, that the girl making me sweep a floor twice would one day be the reason I get up in the morning.

Year One

My turn to clean. You're in charge. I do it once you're not satisfied. I do it twice. No explanation needed, none given.

Year Two, the revenge

Roles flip. Now I'm in charge, and it's your turn. I make you do it twice too. Fair is fair. Neither of us knew we were writing chapter one.

II. Different streets, same silence

Different streams, different lives, no us

After school, our paths split clean in two. Science for me, Commerce for you. Then further still you off to B.Com at SSR College, Silvasa. Me, buried in B.Tech at PDEU, Gandhinagar.

We each had our own lives running in the background, and neither was gentle. There were bad chapters on both sides the kind you don't put in a caption. At one point I wasn't really living, just getting through days. Neither of us was thinking about the other. There was no "us" yet. Just two people surviving their own storms, 353 km apart, with no idea they'd end up in each other's.

III. 3rd December 2024

A birthday text neither of us saw coming

One good day, out of nowhere, I wished you a happy birthday. Nothing planned, nothing calculated. Just a message. And somehow, that one text cracked something open.

From that day the conversation never really stopped. We started sharing our daily routines like it was normal, like we'd always done it. Then it became something else entirely.

IV. the months after

Filterless everything, meant everything

We stopped editing ourselves. Past traumas, secrets neither of us had told another living soul we just handed them to each other, no filter, no fear of what the other would think.

Late night talks that ran too long. Dumb jokes that made no sense to anyone but us. Truth or dare that got a little too honest. You carried my pain and my pointless jokes in the same breath you were the one person who stood by me at my lowest and emotionally pulled me out of the dark. You made me feel comfortable in a way I didn't know I needed. You made me feel special.

Normal friends became best friends became something we hadn't named yet. Unofficially together, acting like it, feeling like it just without the label. Two people who'd unknowingly fallen for each other and were too deep in it to notice the exact moment it happened.

late night calls truth & dare zero filter bad jokes, good therapy secrets we'd never told anyone
V. February 2026

The fight that almost ended us

It wasn't small. A long argument, the kind that leaves silence louder than words. For a moment it felt like this whatever "US" had quietly become was over.

But you didn't let it end. You put in the extra effort when it would've been easier to walk away. You held US together with both hands when I couldn't. That's not a small thing. That's the whole story, actually, condensed into one month.

VI. the turn

And then, you asked

15th March

You proposed. I said yes to the only certainty I've ever fully trusted. From that day, we are together and we will be, for good.

VII. 353 km, always

We never decided to do long distance we just never felt it

We never sat down and made it official "long distance relationship," some label to brace for. We just never felt the 353 km between Vapi and Ahmedabad. Not once. We've never held hands, and somehow our emotional connection operates on a level hands couldn't reach anyway.

Full spectrum of us: fun, cute moments, arguments over nothing, studying together on a call because that counts too, nights that stretched into mornings on video, and yes I love kissing you digitally, screen to screen, because it's not acting and it's not pretending. It's just the closest my lips can get to yours right now, and I mean every one of them and all my love is only for you just for you.

A letter I mean, word for my world

Kira,

I want you to know something plainly, without a joke to hide behind this time: you are the reason I'm happy. Not a reason the reason. You were there when I needed someone most, at a time I wasn't sure I'd come out of, and you stayed. Not for a night. Not for a week. You stayed.

I've thought about this a lot, and I don't think I've earned it. I've been rude to you sharp when I shouldn't have been, distant when you needed me close and you were still there. I've done so little to deserve the way you love me, and yet you keep choosing to anyway, every single day, from 353 km away, like the distance is nothing but a number on a map.

You didn't just supervise a cleaning duty and make my life difficult twice in Year One. You've been supervising my heart ever since, quietly, patiently, making sure I clean up the parts of myself I'd given up on. That's the twist neither of us saw coming back in that classroom.

So love you for the birthday text that started it, for every filterless 2 a.m. conversation, for fighting to keep US alive in February when it would've been easier to let go, for saying yes on the 15th of March, and for loving me more than I've ever given you reason to.

I love you. Not performed. Not typed for effect. Just true.
